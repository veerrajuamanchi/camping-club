## Accounting model and worked evidence

The canonical accounting conventions are in [Financial Worked Examples](FINANCIAL_WORKED_EXAMPLES.md). The five requested cases reconcile in cents:

- Four attendees / `$240` invoice / three `$50` receipts: after allocations and receipt postings, A is owed `$30`; each of B/C/D owes `$10`. Three `$10` transfers clear the invoice exactly.
- Eight attendees / `$250` invoice / eight commitments: A's own commitment is one non-posting booking marker; B–H create seven `$50` receipt events (`$350` cash). Candidate balances are A `−$131.25`, each other member `+$18.75`; seven `$18.75` candidate returns total `$131.25`. No finalization until surplus treatment is approved.
- Post-confirmation withdrawal: `$50` paid by withdrawing D stays non-refundable. Proposed group-credit scenario produces `$13.33` from B and C to A, but is not executable until the owner approves that contribution application.
- Club cancellation: `$240` vendor payment less `$200` provider refund is `$40` documented loss; `$150` member receipts leave `$110` arithmetic remainder. The contributions stay unapplied; no member net transfer is finalized under an unresolved cancellation policy.
- Partial payment + correction: V1 B→A `$30` with `$20` received, then `$30` C-paid fuel expense; V2 residuals are B→A `$10` and C→A `$15`. Old runs/events remain immutable; the `$20` is not requested twice.
