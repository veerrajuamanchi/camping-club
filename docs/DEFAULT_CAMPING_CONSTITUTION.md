# Default Camping Constitution

This is the general-rule copy seeded by Phase 2 and shown in the administrator Constitution view and applicable Coming acknowledgment bundles. The text captures the club's previously provided rules and planning guidance. It does not enable future-phase behavior such as expense approvals, task claims, meal collection, or lottery execution.

## Transportation

- **Driver mileage:** A driver is reimbursed $0.76 per mile when the carpool carries at least three people, including the driver.
- **Car-wash contribution:** Each participant contributes $5 toward the car wash.
- **Van arrangements:** For a group of four, use one van. For five, use one van when feasible; an exception may be used when one van is not feasible. For groups up to eight, aim to use two vans; a third van is an exception.
- **Travel budget target:** Target no more than $50 per person for travel expenses. The $5 per-person car-wash contribution is included in this target.

## Cabin and shared expenses

- **Cabin budget target:** Target no more than $60 per person for cabin expenses. This is a planning target, not an automatic spending cap.
- **Cabin contribution:** Each attendee is responsible for a $50 contribution toward the cabin, paid to the administrator-assigned cabin payer. The trip notice will state the due timing under the approved participation policy. A confirmed attendee's obligation remains after withdrawal; member-withdrawal contributions are non-refundable. The collection workflow remains disabled until its owner-approved policy is finalized.
- **Food budget target:** For a Friday-to-Sunday trip, target no more than $50 per person for shared food and non-alcoholic drinks (for example, $200 for four people). Buy shared food up front within the group target. This is a planning target, not an automatic spending cap.
- **Group expense approval:** Do not charge purchases under the group name without administrator approval. Members may make optional purchases individually; document those expenses and split them only with the people involved.
- **Alcohol cost sharing:** Members should bring their own alcohol or coordinate purchases with interested people. Alcohol is not charged to the group, and a member who does not drink is never charged for it, even if most of the group participates.

## Lodging and responsibilities

- **Floor lottery:** If there are not enough beds for everyone, use a random draw to select the required number of people to sleep on the floor. A selected person may exchange with someone who agrees to swap.
- **Responsibility workload:** Responsibilities include cutting vegetables, cleaning meat, cabin-floor cleaning, bathroom cleaning, dishes, main dish, side dish, curry, making drinks, and activity planning; administrators may add trip-specific tasks. Members choose available responsibilities first-come, first-served during signup; later signups choose from the remaining work. Driving counts toward workload, so drivers should be assigned to members with no or fewer other responsibilities.
- **Responsibility time blocks:** Plan assignments for Friday night, Saturday morning, Saturday night, and Sunday morning. Sunday morning includes cleanup and leaving the cabin tidy.

## Meals and packing

- **Packing checklist:** Pillow, blanket, water shorts, swimming goggles, towel, two or three changes of clothes, toothbrush, toothpaste, optional sleeping bag, shoes, sandals, and sunglasses. Administrators may add trip-specific items.
- **Coffee or tea:** Collect each participant's preference separately for Saturday morning and Sunday morning.
- **Meal preferences:** Collect vegetarian or non-vegetarian preferences for the whole trip or each planned meal. Participants may indicate that they are not eating a particular meal.

## Poll disclosure

- A Coming response records interest and acknowledges the displayed rules. It does not confirm the trip or create a payment obligation.
