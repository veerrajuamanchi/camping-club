Daily target behavior:
- Directory totals include every active template, independently of category/retired visibility filters. The separate volunteer-capacity total multiplies each active weight by its default capacity.
- An admin can opt a trip into a daily workload goal, in 0.5× increments. Existing trips retain their trip-wide quota until configured; the form suggests 4× without automatically applying it.
- Each date sums the selected weights of active duties in enabled slots. Points are counted only on the actual slot date. Targets are met by weight, not by task count.
- A member below target is excused when no eligible slots remain for that date; a day with no scheduled tasks requires no selection. Opening a slot recalculates the member's status. Duplicate claims and capacity limits still apply. RSVP is never blocked by task availability.
- Existing driver-credit facts remain trip-wide and visible separately; they are not duplicated across days or automatically assigned to a daily target.
- Target updates use active administrator resolution, a service-only transactional RPC, optimistic trip version, idempotency and an audit event. No financial rules or money facts change.
